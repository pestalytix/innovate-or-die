A few details will determine whether this is a territory-design issue, a scheduling issue, or a service-design issue:

1. How many field techs and trucks do you operate, and do they start/end from one shop?
2. Are the three towns contiguous—and roughly how dense are your customer clusters within each?
3. Can customers choose or influence their service day/window, and what share are recurring versus reschedules/callbacks?

Even before that: I suspect the missing lever is not better routing; it’s **controlling when and where demand is allowed to exist**. With quarterly accounts, you can redesign recurring service into neighborhood “service cells” and make day assignment part of the product—rather than treating every account as independently schedulable.
