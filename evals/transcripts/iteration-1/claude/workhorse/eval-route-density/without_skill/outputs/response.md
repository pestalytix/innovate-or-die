The software optimizes routes from whatever job list you feed it — but if the underlying scheduling isn't zone-locked, the job list itself is scattered and no algorithm can save it. That's almost always the real lever with quarterly service: 900 accounts / 3 towns / 4 visits a year is inherently thin density, and if customers can book "next available slot" instead of "next available slot in your zone," every reschedule and new signup fragments the routes again.

A few things worth checking before assuming you need better tech:

- **Zone-day discipline**: are specific neighborhoods locked to specific days/weeks, or does the CSR book based on calendar availability? This is usually the single biggest fix — tighter geographic batching, not smarter sequencing.
- **Tech territory ownership**: same tech, same cluster every cycle, vs. techs bouncing between towns week to week.
- **How callbacks/reschedules get inserted** — dispatch often drops these into a route without regard to geography, which is a quiet drive-time killer.
- **New customer acquisition geography** — if sales isn't targeting infill within existing zones, growth itself is diluting density over time.
